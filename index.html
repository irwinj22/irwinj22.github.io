about me


hello! my name is james and i am a graduate computer science student at cal poly, san luis obispo. last year, i completed my bachelor's in computer science with minors in data science and philosophy. now, i am working on my master's thesis with my advisor, dr. john clements. my working thesis title is "understanding the design and impact of digital interfaces for music creation." basically, i want to know how the design of digital systems shapes the sound of music. my areas of study include computer science, computer music, and data science. my work experience has involved machine learning, navigation systems, and in-depth analysis and evaluation of artificial intelligence models. through these opportunities i have been able to apply my technical skills to a number of different problems and domains. i am driven, focused, and eager to learn.
