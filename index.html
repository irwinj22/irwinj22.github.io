about me


hello! my name is james and i am a graduate computer science student at cal poly, san luis obispo. last year, i completed my bachelor's in computer science with minors in data science and philosophy.

now, i am working on my master's thesis with my advisor, dr. john clements. my research explores how the design of digital interfaces shapes the music that people create. basically, i am interested in the relationship between creative tools and outcomes. for my thesis, i plan to design and build a new digital platform for music creation.

outside of my research, i work as a software engineer at northrop grumman, where i am developing software for processing and visualizing 3D flight data in real time. previously, i built and tested a system for transmitting RTK corrections via NTRIP between a base station and a moving receiver.

i am interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence, music technology, and the ways that computing tools shape how people create.
